## 002_不想做事，如何找到动力？

### 第一段：引入场景

**【句式：have always aspired to be... 现在完成时表长期愿望】**
你一直渴望成为一位专业艺术家，终于这个梦想可能成真。
*You've always aspired to be a professional artist. At last, this dream may become a reality.*

**【句式：现在进行时 + to submit不定式作定语】**
你正在制作一个作品集，准备提交给艺术专案。
*You're creating a portfolio to submit to art programs.*

**【句式：as引导时间状语从句 + find yourself + 形容词（宾语补足语）】**
但是随着申请截止日期迫近，你突然发现自己失去动力，完全不想靠近画布。
*But as the application deadline looms, you suddenly find yourself unmotivated, and avoiding the canvas altogether.*

> **语法点**：find yourself + adj. / find yourself doing sth.
> 表示"发现自己处于某种状态"

------

### 第二段：提出问题

**【句式：why does... seem so... + what even is... 双重提问引出主题】**
为什么动机如此善变？动机究竟是什么？
*Why does motivation seem so fickle? And what even is it in the first place?*

> **短语**：in the first place = 究竟、首先

------

### 第三段：定义动机

**【句式：define A as B 定义句型 + 不定式作定语(to initiate and maintain)】**
心理学家将动机定义为启动和持续特定行为的欲望或动力。
*Psychologists define motivation as the desire or impetus to initiate and maintain a particular behavior.*

**【句式：it's the energy that drives you to do something → that引导的定语从句，修饰energy】**
换句话说，动机是驱使你做某件事的能量。
*In other words, it's the energy that drives you to do something.*

**【句式：when it comes to + 动名词（固定句型）】**
想了解如何维持动力，认识动机的来源尤为重要。
*And knowing the source of that drive is particularly important when it comes to understanding how to maintain it.*

**【句式：fall into + 名词（分类句型）】**
动机通常分为两大类：内在动机及外在动机。
*These motivational forces generally fall into two broad categories: intrinsic and extrinsic.*

> **关键词**：intrinsic motivation（内在动机） / extrinsic motivation（外在动机）

------

### 第四段：内在动机详解

**【句式：when引导的时间状语从句，experience A as B（把A体验为B）】**
当你将一个活动本身视为目的时，就涉及到内在动机。
*Intrinsic motivation is involved when you experience an activity as an end in itself.*

**【句式：Take + 名词（举例句型，类似"比如"）】**
以嗜好为例，例如玩电动游戏。
*Take a hobby, like playing video games.*

**【句式：动名词短语作主语】**
经验本身大致就说明了做这件事的欲望来源。
*The experience largely explains the desire to do it.*

**【句式：that引导的定语从句，修饰tasks，并列两个that从句】**
执行当下感觉愉快的任务，或者你觉得有意义、有趣或满足的任务，就是由内在动机驱动的。
*Performing tasks that feel right in the moment—or that you find meaningful, interesting, or satisfying—are driven by intrinsic motivation.*

> **语法点**：两个that引导的定语从句并列，第二个that在从句中作宾语（you find that...）

------

### 第五段：外在动机详解

**【句式：on the other hand（对比连接词）+ refer to A as B（把A定义为B）】**
另一方面，外在动机指的是将一项任务视为达到某种目的的手段。
*Extrinsic motivation, on the other hand, refers to pursuing a task as a means to an end.*

**【句式：while引导的让步状语从句（虽然）】**
虽然很少人认为看牙医是愉快的事，但你的动机通常是想拥有洁净、健康的牙齿。
*While few would consider going to the dentist as an enjoyable activity, you're often motivated by the outcome of having clean, healthy teeth.*

**【句式：whether...or... 列举两种可能】**
外在动机的其他例子包括完成一项任务以获得某种奖励，无论是赞美、权力，还是金钱。
*Other examples of extrinsic motivation include completing a task to receive some sort of reward, whether it's praise, power, or money.*

**【句式：动名词作主语（notably置于句首作状语）】**
值得注意的是，这些奖励通常是之后才会得到，比如在季度结束时获得奖金，或者在几个月的培训后赢得比赛。
*Notably, these rewards tend to come later, like receiving a bonus at the end of a quarter, or winning a competition after months of training.*

> **语法点**：like + 动名词短语，举例说明

------

### 第六段：外在动机的局限性 + 研究发现

**【句式：while引导的让步状语从句 + may seem... but省略】**
虽然得到报酬这种的外在奖励可能看起来很吸引人，但它们的效果可能出奇地短暂。
*While extrinsic rewards, like getting paid, may seem appealing, their effectiveness can be surprisingly short-lived.*

**【句式：those who... 定语从句 + weren't the most likely to... 比较结构】**
例如，2017年的一项研究发现，对新年新希望达成的"结果"特别注重的人，或者受到外在动机驱使的人，并非最有可能坚持下去的人。
*For example, a 2017 study found that those who were highly focused on the outcomes of their New Year's resolutions—or driven by extrinsic motivation—weren't the most likely to stick to them.*

**【句式：what引导的主语从句（what did predict persistence）】**
真正能预测毅力指标的是追求目标时，你感受到多少乐趣。
*What did predict persistence, however, was how much a person enjoyed pursuing their goals.*

**【句式：you're more likely to... than... 比较结构 + rather than 而不是】**
换句话说，如果上的课程是你喜欢的，你比较有可能维持健身的习惯，而不是只去上那些锻炼二头肌的课。
*In other words, you're more likely to maintain an exercise routine if you take classes you enjoy, rather than just those that build your biceps.*

> **定语从句嵌套**：classes (that) you enjoy（省略that作宾语） / those that build your biceps（that作主语不可省略）

**【句式：have shown that... 宾语从句 + be more likely to（更可能）】**
多年的心理学研究显示，对学习、工作或运动课程具有高度内在动机的人更有可能持续长期参与。
*Years of psychology research have shown that high levels of intrinsic motivation—for school, a job, or an exercise class—are more likely to keep you engaged in the long run.*

------

### 第七段：日常活动中的混合动机

**【句式：are rarely either...or... 否定并列（很少是纯粹A或纯粹B）】**
然而，日常活动很少是纯粹内在动机或外在动机。
*Day-to-day actions, though, are rarely either exclusively intrinsically or extrinsically motivated.*

**【句式：if引导的条件状语从句 + be curious about 对……好奇】**
例如，如果你对古埃及文化感到好奇，准备历史考试可能是来自内在动机。
*Studying for a history exam, for example, can be intrinsically motivated if you're curious about the culture of ancient Egypt.*

**【句式：as引导原因状语从句 + feel pressure from... 感到来自……的压力】**
但如果你是想取得好成绩或因家人压力必须得到高分，外在动机就在其中发挥作用。
*But extrinsic motivators may also be at play, as you aim to get a good grade or feel pressure from family members to do well in school.*

> **短语**：at play = 在起作用

------

### 第八段：多重动机的问题（过度合理化效应）

**【句式：having multiple motivators 动名词短语作主语 + isn't always better 比较级】**
但拥有多重动机不一定更好。
*But having multiple motivators isn't always better.*

**【句式：those who... 定语从句 + were driven by... both... and... 并列结构】**
一项针对军校学员的研究发现，那些同时拥有自我提升等内在动机和找到好工作等外在动机驱动的人，总体上比只有一种动机的学员更不积极。
*One study of military cadets found that those who were driven both by intrinsic motivators, like self-improvement, and extrinsic motivators, like the outcome of getting a good job, were overall less motivated than cadets driven by just one of these factors.*

**【句式：结果状语（as a result） + 并列谓语（performed worse and were less likely to...）】**
结果，这些学员表现较差，毕业的可能性也更小。
*As a result, these cadets performed worse and were less likely to graduate.*

**【句式：call A B（把A称为B）+ 同位语解释（the idea that...）】**
心理学家将这种现象称为"过度合理化效应"——也就是当你已拥有做某事的内在动力时，额外的外在激励因素实际上会让状况更加混乱。
*Psychologists call this phenomenon the overjustification effect—the idea that additional extrinsic motivators can actually muddy the waters when you already have the intrinsic drive to do something.*

> **关键短语**：muddy the waters = 搅乱局面、让事情变得更复杂

------

### 第九段：何时使用外在动机

**【句式：this is only a problem if... 条件句 + find + 名词 + 形容词】**
但只有当你已经对某项任务很有兴趣时，这才是问题。
*But this is only a problem if you already find a task motivating.*

**【句式：when you're faced with... 被动结构 + that引导的定语从句修饰activity】**
当你面临一项你觉得单调或无趣的活动时，增加外在奖励可能会有帮助。
*When you're faced with an activity you find tedious or uninteresting, adding extrinsic rewards can be beneficial.*

**【句式：can provide + 名词】**
这样，外在激励因素就可以提供充分的理由。
*In this way, extrinsic motivators can provide sufficient justification.*

**【句式：while引导的让步状语从句 + may never enjoy 可能永远不会喜欢】**
你可能一直都不喜欢洗衣服，但如果会得到亲人的赞许，这件事也许会变得比较容易着手。
*While you may never enjoy doing the laundry, it may feel less daunting if you get praise from a loved one, or even promise yourself that you'll watch your favorite TV show when you're done folding.*

> **宾语从句嵌套**：promise yourself that you'll watch...（that引导宾语从句）+ when引导的时间状语从句

------

### 第十段：总结与建议

动机很复杂。有时，无论你有多热爱某个目标或嗜好，可能都很难找到实际去做的动力。
*Motivation is complicated. And sometimes, no matter how passionate you are about a goal or hobby, finding the motivation to actually do it can be difficult.*

**【句式：even when + 形容词（it feels impossible省略了to find motivation）】**
但即使在最困顿的时刻，也有些方法可以增强你的动力。
*But there are things you can do to increase your drive, even when it feels impossible.*

**【句式：focus on doing sth + by doing sth（方式状语）】**
集中精力去建立内在动机，让任务在执行当下变得更有趣。
*Focus on building intrinsic motivation by making the task more fun in the moment.*

**【句式：动名词短语作主语（asking... or simply putting...）】**
邀请朋友一起加入，或者只是播放你喜欢的音乐，就可以给予你开始启动、坚持目标、长期投入所需的动力。
*Asking a friend to join you or simply putting on your favorite playlist can give you the boost to get started—and stick with your goals for the long haul.*

> **关键短语**：for the long haul = 长期地、持久地
