When you get old and your hair turns grey,
当你老了，头发白了，

Your sleep becomes heavy, and you warm yourself by the fire.
睡意昏沉，炉火旁打盹。

When you get old and you can't walk so fast,
当你老了，走不动了，

Sit by the fire and take out this book, read it slowly,
炉火旁取暖，取下这本书，慢慢读着，

Think back to the soft glow of your eyes in the past,
回想你过去眼神的柔和，

And the shadows of your old, distant dreams.
回想它们昔日浓重的阴影。

How many people loved your happy, charming moments,
多少人曾爱你青春欢畅的时辰，

Loved your beauty, whether true or false, with their whole hearts.
爱慕你的美丽，假意或真心。

But only one person loved the pilgrim soul inside you,
只有一个人还爱你虔诚的灵魂，

Loved the sad lines that time carved on your face.
爱你苍老的脸上的皱纹。

When you bow down, close to the glowing fire,
当你佝偻着，在炉火旁低语，

With a sad whisper, tell yourself of the love that has passed—
凄然地轻轻诉说那爱情的消逝——

It climbed up the hills, wandered among the stars,
在头顶的山上它缓缓踱着步子，

And hid its face in the middle of a crowd of stars.
在一群星星中间隐藏着脸庞。
